A config is a set of cheat settings from your Cloud. Nothing is uploaded by hand: you pick one of
your Cloud configs as the item's source, and every version is a snapshot of it at the moment you
release. Configs aren't reviewed by a person: once the automated checks pass, the item and each
new version are live.

<Note>
  CS2 Inventory configs are skin loadouts and work a bit differently: see
  [Selling CS2 Inventory loadouts](/market/loadouts).
</Note>

---

## Before you start

- **The config in your Cloud**, for the product you'll sell it for. It has to be yours: your own
  config or one gifted to you. A config you bought, or a copy of one, can't be a source.
- **The rules.** Read the [author rules](https://spurdoverse.app/marketplace/rules?tab=authors)
  once.

---

## Step 1: Create the item

Either way works:

- In **Cloud**, open the config's menu and press **Publish on the market**. The draft gets the
  config's name and product, and the config is already its source.
- In **My items**, press **New item**, pick **Config** and the product, then pick the source in
  **Basics** under **Which Cloud config to publish**.

The source can only be a config of the item's product. You can switch to another one of your
configs later: the next version is released from it.

---

## Step 2: Fill in the card

Name, summary, description, tags and at least one screenshot, the same as for a script: see
[The item card](/market/item-card).

**Price & access** takes **Free** or **Forever** only: configs aren't sold by term. A paid plan has
to be at least the market's minimum price, $0.50. See [Prices, discounts and grants](/market/pricing).

---

## Step 3: Publish

Press **Publish** at the bottom of the editor. The server checks the name, the product, the
plans and that there's something to publish. If the item has no version yet, it releases `1.0.0`
to stable from the source config right then.

The automated checks on every version:

| Check | |
|:------|:-|
| Not empty | An empty config isn't released |
| Size | Up to 4 MB |
| JSON | A config that starts like JSON has to be valid JSON |

When they pass, the item is in the catalog at once. If the button refuses, it says why, field by
field: see [When the button refuses](/market/publishing#when-the-button-refuses).

Moderators look at configs only after a report. A config that breaks the
[rules](https://spurdoverse.app/marketplace/rules?tab=authors) is taken down.

---

## Release a new version

Edit the config in Cloud as usual: buyers don't see those edits until you release them. When it's
ready, open **Branches & versions** in the editor and press **Release a version**:

| Field | |
|:------|:-|
| **Config from Cloud** | The source. The version is taken from it as it is now |
| **Branch** | stable, or beta and dev once they're on |
| **Version number** | Optional. Empty means the next patch: `1.0.0` → `1.0.1` |
| **What's changed** | One line per change. Buyers read it in the changelog |

The version is live on its branch right away, and owners on that branch get it as an update.
The number follows the same rules as scripts: semver, higher than the branch's newest version,
never used before in the item, no `-beta` suffix on stable.

---

## Branches

Configs have the same three branches as scripts:

| Branch | Who gets it |
|:-------|:------------|
| **stable** | Every buyer by default. Can't be turned off |
| **beta** | All buyers, or granted only. You pick under **Who can load it** |
| **dev** | Granted only, always |

Turn a branch on in **Branches & versions** before releasing to it. Turning it off moves its users
back to stable. Buyers pick their branch in **My purchases** or in Cloud; testers get a closed
branch through a [grant](/market/pricing#grants).

---

## Roll back and promote

- **Make current** next to an older version of a branch rolls back to it. That makes a new
  version with the next number, the old content and the old changelog, marked "rollback to …".
- **Move to stable** takes a beta or dev version to stable with the same content. The number is
  the version without its suffix (`1.5.0-beta.2` → `1.5.0`) unless you pick another, higher
  than stable's newest.

Neither goes through any review: the content was already checked when it was released.

---

## What buyers get

A bought config shows up in the buyer's Cloud under **From the market** and in the cheat. They
can use it and change it, but not publish or share it. When you release a new version on their
branch, they see the update:

- without edits on their side, the copy just becomes the new version;
- with edits, they choose to replace their edits with your version or keep them;
- **Restore** brings their copy back to the version they're on.

Configs are sold forever only, so a bought config doesn't expire.

---

## Configs from the old market

Configs sold on the old market were moved to the new one as **drafts**, with their versions,
buyers and reviews. The editor and **My items** mark them **Carried over from the old market**
(the **From the old market** filter in **My items** lists them).

- They aren't in the catalog until you publish them. Buyers keep their access meanwhile and see
  "The author has not released it in the new market yet".
- Check the description, the price and the screenshots, then press **Publish**. A price below
  $0.50 has to be raised first.
- If the original Cloud config wasn't found, pick any of your configs of that product as the
  source; without a product, pick it in **Basics**.
- A draft with buyers can't be deleted.
