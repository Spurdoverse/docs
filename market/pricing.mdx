Prices live in the editor's **Price & access** section. They aren't tied to versions: you change
them any time, and the change is live as soon as it's saved.

---

## How it's sold

Under **How it's sold** pick one or both of the paid options, or **Free**:

| Option | | |
|:-------|:-|:-|
| **Free** | Anyone gets it in one click | The only option for libraries |
| **Forever** | One payment, free updates | From $0.50 |
| **By term** | 7, 30 or 90 days with renewal | From $0.50 per plan. Turn on the plans you need |

**Forever** and term plans can be on together: for example $15 forever, or $3 for 7 days. Next to
each price the editor shows **you get** the amount after the market's 20% commission.

### Term plans

- Renewing adds to the remaining time.
- Buyers get a notification 3 days before the end, and can turn on auto-renewal from their balance.
- When the term ends, the script stops loading in the cheat. The buyer's settings stay; they come
  back when the buyer renews.

---

## Changing the price

Edit the price in **Price & access** and press **Save changes**. No new version, no review: the
catalog shows the new price right away.

- Buyers who already own the script keep their access.
- If the price changes while someone is in the purchase window, they see the new price and confirm
  again. Nothing is charged at a price they didn't see.

## Discounts

A discount shows the old price struck through, the percent and the end date, and the item shows up under
the catalog's **On sale** filter and **Biggest discount** sort. Set it in the editor's **Discount** section, or under
**Discounts & promo codes → New discount**.

- 5% to 90%, as a percent or as a new price.
- **From** and **Until** dates. It applies to all plans.
- At most once every 14 days per item: the next discount can start 14 days after this one ends.
  Discounts that aren't real are against the rules.

**Stop** ends a running discount now; a scheduled one is cancelled.

---

## Promo codes

A promo code works only for the people you give it to: your stream, your channel.
**Discounts & promo codes → New promo code**:

| Field | |
|:------|:-|
| **Code** | 4–16 Latin letters and digits, or **Generate** |
| **Discount** | 5–90% |
| **Uses** | A limit, or unlimited |
| **Valid until** | Optional |
| **Which items** | One, several, or **All my paid items** |

A promo code doesn't stack with a discount: the buyer gets whichever is better. Turn a code off
and on again with its switch.

## Grants

A grant gives someone access for free: testers, streamers, compensation. **Grants → Grant access**:

| Field | |
|:------|:-|
| **Item** | Which script |
| **To** | Nick or ID |
| **Term** | 7, 30, 90 days, forever, or until a date |
| **Branches** | Closed branches open just for them: this is how testers get dev |
| **Note to self** | Only you see it |

- 20 grants a month.
- Free for the recipient and commission-free. It doesn't count as a sale.
- The item shows up in their purchases marked **Granted by author**. Their review is marked
  **Granted** and doesn't count toward the rating.
- **Revoke** closes their access; their review stays.

<Warning>
  Mass-granting access to get around sales breaks the author rules.
</Warning>
