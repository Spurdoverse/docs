Your income isn't your site balance. Sales land in a separate income account under
**Income & payouts**, wait out the dispute window, and then you move them to your balance or
withdraw them.

---

## Commission

| Sale | Market takes |
|:-----|:-------------|
| Regular sale | 20% |
| The buyer came through your author link | 10% |

The commission is taken at the moment of sale. The editor shows **you get** next to every price:
that's after commission. Payouts themselves are free.

**Your author link** is on **Income & payouts**: share your profile on social media and streams,
and purchases through it cost you half the commission.

---

## Hold

Each sale stays **On hold** for 7 days: the dispute window. If a buyer gets a refund through a
dispute, it comes out of that sale's hold first. After 7 days the amount moves to
**Available** by itself. **Income & payouts** shows when the next amount comes out of hold.

---

## Getting the money

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Move to balance">
    **Move to balance** puts available income on your site balance instantly, with no minimum.
    Spend it on subscriptions and the market. It can't be moved back to income.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Withdraw">
    **Withdraw** works like referral payouts:

    - from **$20** of available income;
    - methods: **SBP**, **Card**, **USDT**;
    - processed by hand;
    - one request at a time. The amount leaves **Available** when you file it.

    If a request is declined, the money returns to your site balance.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  While a dispute or a rules check is open, the money for those sales stays on hold until it's
  decided. If moderation freezes payouts, **Income & payouts** says so: contact support.
</Note>

## Statistics

The **Author dashboard** shows, for 7, 30, 90 days or all time and per item or branch: income
after commission, sales, card views, conversion from view to purchase, refunds and active users.
**Needs attention** lists reviews waiting for a reply, open disputes, rejected versions and
discounts about to end.
