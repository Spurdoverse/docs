The [market](https://spurdoverse.app/marketplace) sells scripts for spurdo! products. Any spurdo!
account can sell: there's no application and no "become an author" form. You publish with `spd`,
fill in the item's card on the site, and buyers pay from their site balance.

<Note>
  There's no upload button on the site. Every version comes from `spd publish` in your project
  folder: the server builds your sources and checks them. The site only holds the card: name,
  text, screenshots and price.
</Note>

---

## What you can publish

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Script" icon="code">
    Runs in the cheat. Free, forever, or for 7, 30 or 90 days. Its code is private by default:
    buyers run it but can't read it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Open script" icon="lock-open">
    A script with `"visibility": "public"`. Buyers can read its code, see what changed between
    versions and make their own version of it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Library" icon="book">
    Code other scripts import. Always free and open, installed with `spd add`, published under
    the spurdo! library license.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Branches" icon="code-branch">
    Every script and library has stable, beta and dev, each with its own version and changelog.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The details: [Publishing a script](/market/publish), [Libraries](/market/libraries).

## Before you start

- **A project made with `spd new`.** See [Getting started](/).
- **A confirmed email.** `spd login` and `spd publish` refuse without it. Confirm it in the account's
  security settings on the site.
- **Your own code.** Code you bought, or someone's open script whose author doesn't allow forks,
  can't be published as yours.
- **The rules.** Read the [author rules](https://spurdoverse.app/marketplace/rules?tab=authors)
  once: what gets rejected, what discounts are allowed, how disputes work.

## How it goes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    `spd login` once per machine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    `spd publish` in the project folder. The first publish creates the item as a draft and prints
    the link to its editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the item">
    Product, name, summary, description, tags, screenshots, price. See
    [The item card](/market/item-card) and [Prices, discounts and grants](/market/pricing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put it on the market">
    **Send for review** in the editor. A version without `ffi` or `filesystem` permissions is
    live as soon as the build passes; with them, a moderator looks first. See
    [Publishing and moderation](/market/publishing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep it updated">
    Each `spd publish` is a new version. Beta and dev branches, rollbacks and yanks are on
    [Updates and branches](/market/versions).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where everything is

The author's pages are in the market's left column, under **For authors**:

| Page | |
|:-----|:-|
| **Author dashboard** | Income, sales, views, conversion, refunds, and what needs your attention |
| **My items** | Every item you have: drafts, in review, published, rejected |
| **Grants** | Free access for testers, streamers, compensation |
| **Discounts & promo codes** | Running and scheduled discounts, your promo codes |
| **Income & payouts** | Hold, available income, withdrawals |
